System Workflow
1

Ultrasonic Measurement

HC-SR04 emits 40kHz pulses from tank top, timing the return echo to compute air gap distance.

2

Water Height Calculation

Water Level = 21.5 cm − Sensor Distance. Water % = (Water Level / 21.5) × 100.

3

Wi-Fi Transmission & Automation

Sends JSON POST to Node.js backend every 500ms; auto-switches relay & manages dry-run safety.

Complete Wiring Summary (Pin Map)

Follow these physical pin mapping connections for the ESP8266 breadboard setup:

HC-SR04 VCC→ NodeMCU 3V3 / VIN (5V via USB)
HC-SR04 GND→ NodeMCU GND (G)
HC-SR04 TRIG→ D5 (GPIO 14)
HC-SR04 ECHO→ D6 (GPIO 12)
Relay VCC→ NodeMCU VIN (5V)
Relay GND→ NodeMCU GND (G)
Relay IN→ D2 (GPIO 4)
Buzzer + (anode)→ D1 (GPIO 5)
Buzzer − (cathode)→ NodeMCU GND (G)
Pump (via relay)→ External 5V DC supply (COM & NO)
